## What to build

Implement the competitive bidding engine, alternative disposal workflows (donation/recycling), and the distressed analytics dashboard view:
1. **Competitive Bidding**: In the UI, sellers can toggle "Accept Bids" on an inventory item to list it as an offer sheet. The Express backend will asynchronously seed 3 simulated bids in MongoDB (representing different buyers/prices) after a 5-second delay. The UI will display these bids, allowing the seller to click "Award" to sell the batch to the winning buyer.
2. **Alternative Disposal**: Add dropdown options on the inventory item detail to divert the stock to "Donation" or "Recycling". Triggers Express endpoints to update the item status and log simulated logistics events (e.g., "Food bank notified", "Dispatcher matched", "Freight route locked") to a transaction timeline.
3. **Distressed Analytics Tab**: Build a dedicated analytics tab/view on the dashboard displaying pre-computed metrics and charts, including COGS Recovery Rate, Waste Diverted (tons), and landfill fees saved, updating dynamically as mock transactions, donations, and bids are completed.
