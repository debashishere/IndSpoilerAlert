# 58. Sales & Clearing Leaderboard Tab Consolidation

To prevent duplicate data presentation between Overview & Analytics and standalone drilldowns, Sales & Clearing navigation is structured into two primary tabs: "Overview & Analytics" and "Leaderboard". The duplicate static leaderboard card in Overview is removed, and the "Leaderboard" tab houses dedicated secondary sub-tabs ("Lead Buyers" and "Lead Warehouses") that render dynamic partner rankings and actionable expandable transaction ledgers in a single authoritative location.
