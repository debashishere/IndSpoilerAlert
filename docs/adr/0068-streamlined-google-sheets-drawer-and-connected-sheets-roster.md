# 68. Streamlined Google Sheets Drawer and Connected Sheets Roster

To eliminate misleading static inputs that fail on private spreadsheets, the Google Sheets Configuration Drawer is redesigned into two focused sections: (1) a Master Apps Script Setup Guide with one-click copy and actionable setup steps, and (2) a live Connected Sheets Roster displaying discovered spreadsheets with their sync status, lot counts, and mapping actions. The obsolete Google Workspace OAuth section and manual spreadsheet coordinate input fields are completely removed.
